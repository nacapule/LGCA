what am I looking at? (fidelity notes)
  • The model. Each lattice site has 6 channels, one per hexagonal direction. Every step: (1) collision — each site re-picks its channel occupations, conserving its particle count; (2) streaming — every particle hops exactly one site along its channel. There is no rest channel: everything moves at speed 1, every step.
  • Fermion: at most one particle per channel; collision = 12 Metropolis permutation proposals, energy = sens × polar alignment with the 6 neighbours. Boson: unlimited occupancy; every particle re-draws its channel each step from a Boltzmann law built (by default) from the site's own nematic field.
  • Motion. The model updates at integer steps: particles hop one site along their channel after collision. Interpolated view draws a constant-speed glide between those endpoints; it is a way to show the discrete hop, not an extra rule or a tracked particle identity. Raw states shows the exact streamed lattice at each integer step.
  • Physics toggles. Defaults marked (C++) reproduce lgca_clean-1.cpp exactly. kernel: average divides the alignment field by the particle count that built it (removing the explicit crowd-size factor at fixed directional composition). The combined average + neighbours + polar settings reproduce the active boson collision rule in lgca_noib.cpp; they do not reproduce that older program's different RNG initialization. power α is a research extension: divide by Mα, with exact sum at α=0 and average at α=1. The neighbours only field excludes the centre; site + neighbours includes all seven sites in both the field and the normalization count M. Any non-default toggle is a labelled departure from lgca_clean-1.cpp.
  • Random-stream equivalence. Same seed + same settings uses the same WELL1024a draws and produces the same states as the corresponding C++ build (MODEL=FERMION or BOSON), verified state-for-state by verify-parity.mjs. Changing sensitivity/kernel mid-run keeps the stated transition rule but is a parameter-change experiment no single C++ run performs. Cross-platform caveat: browser and C++ exp/log libraries can differ in the last bit, so an exceptionally near-threshold random draw could make bitwise trajectories diverge even though the stochastic dynamics are the same.
  • Replay & time travel. The tool keeps the recent post-collision states in memory. The replay slider scrubs through them view-only (press live to snap back to the newest state). The transport buttons move the actual state: −1/−N restore the exact past lattice and the random-number generator, so stepping forward again reproduces the identical trajectory — unless you change a parameter after rewinding, which starts a deliberate counterfactual branch. +N computes ahead (committing first at the scrub position if you were replaying). Back-stepping is limited to the buffered window. Tip: , then . re-runs a single tick with its glide animated.
  • Order parameters (0→1 for a nonempty lattice; N = total particles, channel k has unit vector ck at angle θk = 60°·k, nk = channel occupancy). Polar, nematic and spatial follow the C++; band includes the channel omitted by the reference:
    • polar = |Σ nkck|/N over all sites and channels — the length of the average velocity vector. Opposite directions cancel; 1 ⇔ everyone moves the same way.
    • nematic = |Σ nk(cos 2θk, sin 2θk)|/N — same sum with doubled angles, so θ and θ+180° add instead of cancelling: measures a shared axis. A perfect flock also scores 1 here.
    • spatial = 1 − S/ln(W·H), where S = −Σoccupied sites p ln p and p = (site population)/N is the Shannon entropy of where particles sit. Even spread → S = ln(W·H) → 0; all particles on one site → 1.
    • band: for each occupied site, sum the doubled-angle vectors of the populations at distances 1–4 along the 6 lattice directions (direction of displacement, not velocity). Divide the vector length by the sampled population, weight by the central site's population, then average over N. High values mean nearby mass lies mostly along one axis. The Lab counts all six channels; lgca_clean-1.cpp omits channel 0. This deliberate correction affects only the measurement, never the dynamics.
  • Keys: space play/pause · , / . one step back / ahead (ahead animates the glide) · [ / ] jump −N / +N · 0 reset view.
  • Console API: runBatch({model,tsteps,iters,sens,dens,seed,W,H}) mirrors the C++ realization loop (one RNG stream) and prints its output format with the corrected band measurement; dumpState({...}) emits the raw lattice for validation.
